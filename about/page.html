About tiki.boats

Q & A

What is this website?

Tiki.boats is a website made by two people, for fun. This website contains lots of miscellaneous projects, including games, articles, and other interactive things.

This page was originally created in 2022, and has gone through many changes and additions since then. Since 2022, many different pages have been added, most of which are listed in the directory. However, there are some “secret” pages that may be harder to find, and a few pages that aren’t linked to anywhere at all.

What is a “tiki fish”?

Scroll up.

It’s an average-sized tropical fish with green and gold stripes, and cyan translucent fins.

Where are all of the older pages?

In mid-2023, tiki.boats was completely redesigned, and most old pages were moved to tiki.boats/legacy/<page url> in the process. All pages that were moved are still listed in the new directory under the Legacy pages section, so you can still find any page from the old site there.
