About

Tiki.boats is a website made by a person or two, for fun, because why not.

It's basically an ever-growing collection of random small projects (and a few larger ones sprinkled in) with no real overarching theme. You can find a list of all of these projects (except a few secrets) on the directory. The link for that should be in the navbar at the top, along with links to a few other pages, and the logo which will always take you back to the homepage.

I'm not really sure why I created this site, as there's no real reason why any of these things need to be on the internet. Speaking of creating things for no reason, I don't know why I'm even bothering to write all of this, because I'm probably one of about three people who will ever visit this about page. Anyways, we hope you enjoy your flight, and thank you for choosing tiki.boats airlines.
